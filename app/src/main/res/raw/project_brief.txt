SERVICE PAGE WEBSITE - PROJECT BRIEF

Goal
Create a page that displays the services offered by the company, using a blue and red colour scheme.

Scope
1. A hero section with a short pitch and a call-to-action button.
2. A grid of service cards, each showing a short description and price information.
3. A responsive layout that works on phones, tablets and desktops.
4. A contact form with basic validation.

Out of scope
- Online payments
- User accounts

Deadline: end of the month.
